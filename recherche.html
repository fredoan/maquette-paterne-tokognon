"Innovation Policy for Sustainable Development of SMEs in Benin"
4th International Conference on Green Technology and Sustainable Development (GTSD)
IEEE, 2018 — pp. 511–517
📅2018
📍IEEE GTSD Conference
📄pp. 511–517
Cette étude examine les politiques d'innovation et leur rôle dans le développement durable des PME béninoises. Elle propose un cadre de politique d'innovation adapté au contexte africain, en analysant les obstacles structurels et les leviers institutionnels qui conditionnent la performance des petites et moyennes entreprises au Bénin. This study examines innovation policies and their role in the sustainable development of Beninese SMEs. It proposes an innovation policy framework adapted to the African context, analyzing the structural barriers and institutional levers that condition the performance of small and medium enterprises in Benin.

"Impact of Social Ties and Institutional Support System on the Performance of SMEs: An Empirical Study from Benin"
3rd Technology Innovation Management and Engineering Science — International Conference (TIMES-iCON 2018)
IEEE, 2018 — pp. 1–4
📅2018
📍TIMES-iCON · IEEE
📄pp. 1–4
Cette étude empirique analyse l'impact des liens sociaux et du système de soutien institutionnel sur la performance des PME béninoises. Les résultats révèlent le rôle déterminant du capital social et des réseaux institutionnels dans la croissance et la résilience des entreprises en contexte africain. This empirical study analyzes the impact of social ties and institutional support systems on the performance of Beninese SMEs. Results reveal the determining role of social capital and institutional networks in enterprise growth and resilience in the African context.

This publication questions the role of innovation policies in building sustainable micro and small enterprises in Benin. The article offers a comparative analysis of existing policies and formulates concrete recommendations for Beninese decision-makers seeking to stimulate sustainable entrepreneurship.

"Service Innovation and TQM: A Conceptual Framework of Customer Satisfaction"
MSMI 2014 Conference Proceeding
2014 — pp. 167–171
📅2014
📍MSMI Conference
📄pp. 167–171
Ce travail propose un cadre conceptuel reliant l'innovation de services et le Management de la Qualité Totale (TQM) à la satisfaction client. Il établit les fondements théoriques d'un modèle intégré permettant aux organisations d'améliorer simultanément leur performance qualité et leur attractivité client. This work proposes a conceptual framework linking service innovation and Total Quality Management (TQM) to customer satisfaction. It establishes the theoretical foundations of an integrated model enabling organizations to simultaneously improve quality performance and customer attractiveness.
